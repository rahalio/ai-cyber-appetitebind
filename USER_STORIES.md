# Appetitebind — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Chief risk officer / Head of ERM

- As the CRO, I want every production AI use case bound to appetite criteria I can defend to the board, so that AI adoption expands inside limits rather than beside them.
- As the CRO, I want predicted appetite breaches surfaced before the committee pack is locked, so that governance is anticipatory rather than forensic.
- As the CRO, I want a single inventory of AI applications with owners, residual risk, and kill-switch drill status, so that supervisory questions do not trigger a scavenger hunt.

### AI use-case owner (e.g. pricing / underwriting)

- As a use-case owner, I want the assessment criteria visible before I build, so that I do not discover conduct constraints at go-live.
- As a use-case owner, I want variances versus the reference pricing model explained at driver level, so that I can challenge or accept AI weights with underwriters.
- As a use-case owner, I want a hand-to-human path when confidence is outside tolerance, so that difficult cases do not force an unsafe automated decision.

### Risk / compliance partner

- As a risk partner, I want to embed assess and control checkpoints into the agile lifecycle for high-risk cases, so that waterfall GRC gates are not the only option.
- As a compliance partner, I want fairness thresholds tested on out-of-sample and live monitoring data, so that policy is measurable rather than aspirational.
- As a compliance partner, I want GDPR-relevant automated decisions to carry an explanation artefact suitable for a meaningful customer response, so that opacity does not become a regulatory incident.

### Conduct / fairness specialist

- As a fairness specialist, I want rejection and pricing outcomes monitored by protected and vulnerable segments, so that discriminatory drift is visible in portfolio mix and complaints.
- As a fairness specialist, I want edge-case pairs near the decision boundary reviewed by humans, so that feedback corrections enter the model under governance.

### Internal audit / regulatory relations

- As an internal auditor, I want to test whether kill-switches were drilled and whether three-lines sign-off occurred at required stages, so that I audit the RMF rather than re-perform data science.
- As a regulatory relations lead, I want an exportable pack of inventory, appetite bindings, test evidence, issues, and monitoring breaches, so that supervisory engagement is evidence-led.
- As an auditor, I want algorithm variations versioned with who changed what and why, so that continuous learning does not erase accountability.
